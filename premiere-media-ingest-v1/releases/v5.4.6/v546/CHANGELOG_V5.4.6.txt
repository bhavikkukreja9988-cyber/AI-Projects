V5.4.6

Root cause found and fixed for the Stack-insertion failure that
persisted through V5.4.5's attempted fix ("No free track space
available at position 0.00s" / "...at or after 0.00s").

The V5.4.5 fix added a fallback search when the exact playhead has no
free track, but the FIRST search (checking the exact playhead before
falling back) had two real bugs that could make it wrongly report
"nothing free" even when free tracks genuinely existed:

1. If your saved/selected Video or Audio Track preference pointed to
   a track index that doesn't exist in the CURRENT sequence (e.g. you
   had V3 selected from a previous project, now working in a sequence
   with only 2 video tracks), the track scan started above the actual
   track count and exited immediately without checking anything --
   silently treating a totally free V1/V2 as "occupied."

2. The scan only checked tracks at or above your preferred index,
   never below it. If your preferred track was occupied but a lower
   track was free at the exact same playhead position, it would skip
   past that free track and fall through to searching for a LATER
   position in time instead -- unnecessary and not what "Stack at
   Playhead" is supposed to do.

Fixed: the track scan now tries your preferred track first (if it's
valid for the current sequence), then checks every other track from
V1/A1 upward. Free space at the exact playhead is now always found if
it exists, on whatever track it's on.

Also added: if the "no free track space" error is ever hit again for
a genuinely different reason, the message now reports the actual
video/audio track count and stream mode, e.g.:

  "No free track space available at or after 0.00s. (sequence has 1
  video track(s), 1 audio track(s), stream mode: both). Add a track
  in Premiere and try again."

instead of just "No free track space available at 0.00s." -- so if
it recurs, the next report will say exactly why instead of needing
another round of guessing.

No other files touched. No settings reset -- this only changes the
internal track-search algorithm, not what's saved or how it's stored.

WHAT TO TEST:
1. The exact scenario that failed before: Stack mode, playhead at the
   very start of the timeline (0:00), with existing clips already on
   some tracks. Confirm the new clip lands on the first genuinely free
   track instead of erroring.
2. If you have a saved Video/Audio Track preference set to something
   other than "Auto," try it in a sequence with fewer tracks than
   that preference (e.g. preference = V3, sequence only has V1/V2)
   and confirm it still finds free space instead of failing.
3. If it fails again for any reason, the new error message will
   report actual track counts -- please paste that exact message,
   it'll make the next fix much faster.
